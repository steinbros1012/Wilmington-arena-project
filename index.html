Our mission

The Wilmington Arena Project is a community-driven initiative with the goal of developing a multipurpose arena that serves our community through sport, education, and shared experiences. Providing an inclusive hub for athletics, cultural events, youth engagement, and community connection that enhances quality of life for all residents.

03

A community-driven initiative to develop a multipurpose arena that serves our community through sport, education and shared experiences.

The vision

A 10,000-seat multipurpose arena for Wilmington, NC.

As a community-owned nonprofit founded in 2024, our vision is to uplift our youth through sports, education programs and health. Housing professional ice hockey and basketball, the arena will increase community togetherness and boost our local economy.

The work today

Investing in the community now.

While we work toward that long-term vision, we run grassroots programming, youth initiatives, mobile activations, community events and public watch parties that bring people together, promote healthy lifestyles and strengthen community pride throughout the Cape Fear region.

Meet theleaders.

Lifelong friends, local advocates and a world-class coach, united to build a lasting home for sport in Wilmington.

Hover to see him on the ice
President & Founder, Chairman of the Board

Michael “Mike” Völlmin

Mike grew up in Wilmington and found his passion for hockey when Ogden’s Wilmington Ice House opened in 2006. After earning a finance degree and playing hockey at Babson College, he went on to a decade-long pro career in Switzerland. Knowing how hockey opened doors in his life, he launched the project to give back to his hometown.
